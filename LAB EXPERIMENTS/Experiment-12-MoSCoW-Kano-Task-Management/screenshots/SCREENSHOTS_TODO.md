### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Open Jira Software Cloud (`https://id.atlassian.com/login`).
2. Create Scrum project `"Task Management System"` (Key: `TMS1`).
3. Under **Backlog**, add the 4 tasks:
   - `User Login and Role Assignment`
   - `Task Creation and Assignment`
   - `Task Prioritization and Deadlines`
   - `Progress Tracking and Reporting`
4. Add MoSCoW and Kano labels:
   - For `User Login`: labels = `MoSCoW-Must`, `Kano-Basic`
   - For `Task Creation`: labels = `MoSCoW-Must`, `Kano-Basic`
   - For `Task Prioritization`: labels = `MoSCoW-Should`, `Kano-Performance`
   - For `Progress Tracking`: labels = `MoSCoW-Could`, `Kano-Excitement`
5. Create sprint `TMS1 Sprint 1`, move all 4 tasks, and click **Start Sprint**.
6. Move `Task Creation` to In Progress, `Task Prioritization` to Done, and capture Figures 12.5 and 12.6.
7. Save images into this `screenshots/` directory.
