## Key Planning Metrics
1. **Nominal Team Capacity:** 7 engineers × 10 days × 6 hours = 420 nominal hours.
2. **Net Focused Capacity:** 420 × 0.80 = 336 focused engineering hours per sprint.
3. **Sprint 1 Commitment:** Exactly 16 Story Points ($3+5+2+3+3$), meeting the initial 16-point ceiling.
4. **WIP Limits:** In Progress (4), Review (2), Test (2), reflecting the constraint of a single QA engineer.
