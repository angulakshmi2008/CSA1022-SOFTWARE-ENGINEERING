# Stakeholders, User Roles, and Permission Boundaries

## 1. Overview
The Smart Campus Facility Booking and Maintenance Platform integrates operational management across campus teaching spaces, specialized laboratories, sensor telemetry, maintenance requests, and administrative oversight. The platform enforces strict server-side Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). No client-side role assertion is trusted.

## 2. Stakeholder Profiles and Access Matrix

| Role / Actor | Core Responsibilities | Permitted Actions | Access & Security Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Student** | Browse facility availability calendar; submit regular space bookings; request permitted equipment loans; view status of own requests. | `GET /api/facilities/`<br>`GET /api/bookings/calendar/`<br>`POST /api/bookings/` (standard spaces only)<br>`POST /api/equipment-loans/`<br>`GET /api/bookings/my-bookings/` | Strictly restricted to public facilities. Cannot book restricted spaces without approval. Cannot approve requests, access maintenance queues, or view other users' private booking details. |
| **Faculty Member** | Reserve teaching lecture halls, classrooms, and academic laboratories; report maintenance issues manually; track own departmental bookings. | `GET /api/facilities/`<br>`GET /api/bookings/calendar/`<br>`POST /api/bookings/` (teaching spaces & labs)<br>`POST /api/tickets/` (manual reports)<br>`PATCH /api/bookings/{id}/cancel/` | Can book departmental and general academic facilities. May initiate requests for restricted research facilities subject to approver review. |
| **Facility Manager** | Oversee room allocations, track campus SLA compliance, review explainable triage recommendations, assign maintenance tickets to technicians, and coordinate room maintenance closures. | `GET /api/dashboard/`<br>`GET /api/tickets/`<br>`PATCH /api/tickets/{id}/assign/`<br>`PATCH /api/tickets/{id}/triage/`<br>`GET /api/sla-policies/`<br>`PATCH /api/facilities/{id}/status/` | Full administrative visibility across assigned campus facilities and maintenance workorders. Cannot override database audit logs. |
| **Maintenance Technician** | Inspect reported faults, accept assigned tickets, log timestamped inspection notes, request restricted change approvals, submit resolution notes, and mark work as resolved. | `GET /api/tickets/assigned/`<br>`PATCH /api/tickets/{id}/accept/`<br>`POST /api/tickets/{id}/inspections/`<br>`PATCH /api/tickets/{id}/resolve/` | Can only update tickets explicitly assigned to them. Cannot unilaterally close tickets requiring administrative verification. Cannot bypass resolution note requirements (enforced with HTTP 422). |
| **Department Approver** | Evaluate and decide requests for restricted facilities (e.g., High-Containment Bio-Chem Lab, Cleanroom, Advanced Robotics Lab) and high-impact maintenance operations. | `GET /api/approvals/pending/`<br>`POST /api/approvals/` (`decision: approve/reject`, `reason: string`) | Authority is strictly scoped to delegated approval queues. Every approval or rejection requires a mandatory justification and generates an immutable audit event. |
| **System Administrator** | Manage master user roles, facility inventory, campus registrations, SLA policies, and review tamper-evident audit history. | Full CRUD access across `/api/admin/`, `/api/users/`, `/api/campuses/`, `/api/facilities/`, `/api/sla-policies/`, `/api/audit-events/` | Operations are comprehensively logged with actor ID, IP address, timestamp, and before/after state snapshots. |
| **Sensor / Telemetry Client** | Automated campus IoT devices (e.g., temperature, HVAC vibration, acoustic noise, power meter) and external systems delivering telemetry events. | `POST /api/sensor-events/`<br>`POST /api/integration-intents/` | Authenticated via dedicated service tokens. Must submit unique `(source_id, event_id)` tuples. Suppresses duplicate replays deterministically. |

## 3. Server-Enforced Permission Rules
1. **Principle of Least Privilege:** Endpoints default to denying unauthenticated requests (`rest_framework.permissions.IsAuthenticated`). Specific operational actions require granular permission classes (`IsFacilityManager`, `IsDepartmentApprover`, `IsAssignedTechnician`).
2. **Authoritative Identity Derivation:** Requester identities are extracted directly from the authenticated session/token (`request.user.id`). Client-supplied user ID parameters are rejected or ignored.
3. **Guard Against Role Elevation:** Role switches presented in demonstration user interfaces operate strictly as demonstration client perspectives; the backend re-validates the requesting identity against database-stored user attributes on every API transaction.
